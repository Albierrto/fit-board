# Stylebook

This is the board's working style knowledge. Each hunt reads it first and adds what it learned at the bottom. Over time it should read like the notes of a really good stylist who knows Bort's closet. Keep entries short and concrete.

## What Bort likes

- Affordable but genuinely stylish. Lots of options, each with a picture.
- **"Zany" means** weird designer-ish pieces and loud color or print that still look good, but aren't traditional. Marni (especially Marni x Uniqlo), Engineered Garments, Needles, Homme Plissé, Kenzo and loud vintage knits all landed well.
- He loved a batch of cozy, slightly dated knits: striped cardigans, fuzzy mohair, 3D-knit grandpa sweaters, and print shirts. He said they gave Project Hail Mary vibes (Ryland Grace's fox cardigan, striped sweaters, chambray, and a look that "does not look styled"). That vibe is one flavor he likes. It is not a theme the board has to follow.
- He likes oversized shirts.
- Secondhand is fine, from any site.
- Style references should come from people who actually have it.

## What he doesn't want

- Jeans-heavy outfits. He rejected a "Jonah Hill fit".
- Costume-y weirdness. Zany still has to look good on a real person.
- Expensive picks when a cheaper version of the same idea exists.

## His closet (build around it)

- **Tops:**
  - Oversized brown tee and brown sweater (Old Navy).
  - Honey and dusty blue oversized long sleeves (Old Navy).
- **Pants:**
  - Light blue and dark navy wide wool pants (Uniqlo).
  - Olive and black ripstop drawstring pants (All in Motion).
  - Tan baggy workwear pants (Old Navy).
  - Blue jeans and black jeans. Use these sparingly.
- **Jacket:** a black Columbia zip jacket, regular fit.
- **Shoes:** white sneakers and brown Uggs.

## Principles the board uses

- **Size to skim, not cling.** Oversized tops with straight, wide or barrel pants. Hem pants so they just touch the shoe.
- **Heavier fabric drapes better:** heavy cotton, cord, wool, canvas, mohair.
- **Wear layers open** so they make a long line down the middle.
- **One loud thing per outfit.** Let the closet basics calm it down.
- **One color family reads clean:** all blues, all browns, all black.
- **Good pairings with his closet:** brown with light blue, honey with navy, camel with blue, and olive with brown or honey.
- **Shoes:** a chunky shoe under loose pants, so the shoe doesn't look tiny.

## Stylist sources worth studying

- **Writers and editors:**
  - Derek Guy (Die, Workwear! and @dieworkwear)
  - Put This On
  - Permanent Style
  - Throwing Fits
  - GQ and Esquire style sections
  - Highsnobiety
  - Ivy Style
- **Japanese magazines:** Popeye and Free & Easy. Great for relaxed, layered, slightly odd combos.
- **Street style:** Pitti Uomo and the fashion weeks. Look at the regular guys photographed there, not only the runway.
- **Brand lookbooks** show how the brand styles its own pieces. Useful ones: Marni, Engineered Garments, Kapital, Beams Plus, Story mfg., Universal Works, Bode, Noah, Our Legacy.
