How your clothes should fit

  • Size for your widest point. If a tee pulls anywhere, go up a size. Clothes should skim, not cling.
  • Heavier fabric is better. Heavy cotton, cord, wool and canvas drape. Thin stuff shows everything.
  • Wear layers open. An open jacket, cardigan or overshirt makes a long line down the middle.
  • Straight or loose pants. Get them hemmed ($10 to $20) so they just touch your shoes.
  • One color family reads taller. All blues, all browns, all black. No hard break at the waist.
  • One loud thing per outfit. A bright color or a wild print, never two.
